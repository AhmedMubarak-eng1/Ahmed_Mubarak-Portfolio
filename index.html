About Me

Built for the field.
Driven by purpose.

I'm Ahmed Mubarak, an Instrumentation and Controls Engineer at AECOM. I support engineering scopes from design development through FAT, SAT, commissioning, and closeout, coordinating technical requirements, documents, schedules, open actions, vendors, contractors, clients, and multidisciplinary design inputs.

My controls background includes PLC programming, HMI/SCADA development, control-panel design, instrumentation, I/O documentation, electrical schematics, field verification, and troubleshooting. Earlier roles with GE Vernova, JNE Group, Midwest Services Canada, and Salford Group added experience in power-generation outages, steel manufacturing, building automation, and mobile-equipment electrical design.

I bring both a technical controls perspective and a project-delivery mindset: clear documentation, disciplined follow-through, practical field awareness, and alignment across safety, quality, scope, schedule, and stakeholder expectations.
