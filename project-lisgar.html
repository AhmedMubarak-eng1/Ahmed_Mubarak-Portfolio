Municipal Infrastructure · I&C · FAT/SAT

Lisgar District – Doug Leavens Pumping Station

Controls verification connecting engineering documents, control panels, PLC/SCADA software, field instrumentation, and site equipment.

AECOM · Municipal water infrastructure 2026 I&C engineering and commissioning support

Project Overview

Supported the I&C verification and commissioning lifecycle for a municipal pumping station. The work required traceability from design intent and panel documentation through software testing, field I/O verification, equipment-response checks, deficiency tracking, and coordinated closeout.

Engineering Contribution

  • Reviewed I&C shop drawings, IFC electrical and controls drawings, P&IDs, specifications, panel documentation, and the commissioning plan to understand functional requirements and testing interfaces.
  • Prepared and reviewed SCADA/HMI screen packages, I/O-check forms, functional test cases, comments, and revision records for structured verification.
  • Supported control-panel FAT activities by checking panel construction and documentation against project requirements, capturing redlines, deficiencies, and follow-up actions.
  • Supported software FAT activities covering operator displays, signals, alarms, commands, interlocks, equipment status, and expected process responses.
  • Participated in SAT and commissioning activities, including end-to-end SCADA I/O checks, level-instrument verification, pump command/status testing, alarm and feedback checks, troubleshooting, and retesting.
  • Coordinated findings with project management, engineering, quality, the contractor, controls integrator, vendors, owner/operator representatives, and site personnel.
  • Supported preparation of testing records, deficiency documentation, commissioning reports, and outstanding-item tracking for final resolution.

Verification Lifecycle

01 · ReviewDesign & documentation

P&IDs, drawings, specifications, shop drawings, control narratives, and commissioning requirements.

02 · FATPanel & software testing

Panel verification, redlines, HMI/SCADA screens, test cases, alarms, interlocks, and documented findings.

03 · SATField & system verification

SCADA I/O, field instruments, equipment commands and feedback, troubleshooting, deficiency tracking, and retesting.

Engineering Value

The assignment strengthened practical experience across the complete controls-acceptance workflow: converting design requirements into traceable test evidence, identifying discrepancies, coordinating corrective actions, and verifying field behavior across electrical, instrumentation, PLC, SCADA/HMI, and mechanical interfaces.

Public-summary boundary: this case study intentionally excludes infrastructure photographs, drawings, panel identifiers, detailed I/O information, test records, deficiencies, and other project-sensitive material.
