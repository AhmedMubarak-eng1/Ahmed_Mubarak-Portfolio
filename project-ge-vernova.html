GE Vernova – Ontario Power-Station Outage Program

GE Vernova (General Electric) Jan – Dec 2024 Ontario, Canada

Overview

Worked in a combined project-coordination and electrical-controls field-engineering capacity supporting outage projects across five Ontario power stations. The program covered gas turbines, steam turbines, generators, excitation systems, electrical controls, and DCS-related field work from planning through testing, commissioning, and closeout.

The role connected technical execution with project delivery: coordinating schedules and daily priorities, tracking progress and action items, reviewing project records, and working alongside project managers, engineers, client representatives, vendors, contractors, and site teams.

Controls & Commissioning Support

Supported troubleshooting and validation of electrical controls and DCS systems by reviewing field signals, alarms, control logic, operator-interface information, and equipment status. Field activities included loop checks, signal verification, instrumentation troubleshooting, control-system validation, and commissioning support on live outage projects.

Control-room environment used during system review, troubleshooting, and validation.

From Field Signal to Operator Display

The work required following control information from field instruments and equipment through the control system to operator displays and alarms. This strengthened my ability to connect drawings, field conditions, system behavior, and commissioning records when resolving issues.

Outage Execution & Technical Coordination

Supported inspections, maintenance, repairs, testing, and commissioning activities for turbine and generator systems alongside senior engineers and specialist site teams. Reviewed engineering drawings, vendor documentation, commissioning records, and field reports to support issue tracking, risk identification, progress reporting, and resolution of field challenges.

Field engineering assignment during the Ontario outage program.
